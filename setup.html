Set up built-in calling

After this, you click Call in the dialer and talk through your headset. No phone needed. It takes about 20 minutes, done once on your Windows PC in Chrome or Edge.

How it works

Twilio is a calling service that rents you a business phone number. The dialer page connects to Twilio over the internet and Twilio places the call. Three small pieces of code, called Functions, run inside your Twilio account and keep your Twilio password out of the web page. A PIN that only you know protects them, so nobody else can place calls on your account.

Steps

  1. Create a Twilio account and upgrade it

    Sign up at twilio.com/try-twilio. Then open your account menu (top right) and choose Upgrade account, adding a card and the first $20.

    Trial accounts can only call phone numbers you have verified, and they play a "trial account" message to everyone, so the upgrade is needed for real calls.

  2. Buy a phone number

    In the Twilio Console go to Phone Numbers → Manage → Buy a number. Pick United States, tick Voice, and search by the area code your leads will recognize. Buy one local number.

  3. Create an API key

    Go to Account menu → API keys & tokens → Create API key. Name it call-list-dialer and keep the type Standard.

    Copy the SID (starts with SK) and the Secret into a note for step 6. Twilio shows the secret only once.

  4. Create a Functions service

    Go to Functions and Assets → Services → Create Service and name it call-list-dialer. If you don't see Functions and Assets in the left menu, type "Functions" in the Console search bar.

  5. Add the three Functions

    In the service, click Add + → Add Function three times. Name each one exactly as below, paste its code over the sample code, set its visibility with the dropdown next to its name, and click Save.

  6. Add your settings

    Click Settings & More → Environment Variables. Leave "Add my Twilio Credentials (ACCOUNT_SID) and (AUTH_TOKEN) to ENV" ticked, then add these:

    KeyValue
    API_KEYThe SID from step 3 (starts with SK)
    API_SECRETThe secret from step 3
    DIALER_PINA long passcode only you know. Make one here:
    —
    Save it in your note too. You'll type it into the dialer in step 9.
    CALLER_IDOptional. The number from step 2 in +15551234567 form. Without it, the dialer uses the first number on your account.

  7. Deploy

    Click Deploy All at the bottom and wait for "Deployed to environment". Then click the /token function and use Copy URL at the bottom right. Keep only the start of it, without /token, for example https://call-list-dialer-1234.twil.io.

  8. Allow calls to the US and Canada only

    Go to Voice → Settings → Geo permissions. Make sure United States & Canada is on and other countries are off. The /voice Function already refuses anything else, and this adds a second lock against expensive international calls.

  9. Connect the dialer

    Open the dialer, open Columns & dialing settings, and set Call using to Built-in calling. Paste the address from step 7 and your PIN, then click Connect. You should see "Connected" and your Twilio number.

    Make the first call to your own cell phone. Chrome or Edge will ask to use your microphone. Click Allow.

Keep your calls from showing as "Spam Likely"

New numbers that make many calls often get labeled as spam by mobile carriers, which cuts answer rates. Registering your business with Twilio helps. Do this in the first week:

  • Trust Hub → Customer Profiles: create your Primary Business Profile (business name, EIN, address, authorized contact) and submit it. Review can take a few business days.
  • After approval, in Trust Hub add your number to SHAKEN/STIR Trusted Calling. Carriers then see your calls as verified.
  • Also register the number for Voice Integrity. Twilio registers it with the carriers' spam-screening services.
  • Keep each number's daily volume reasonable. If one number gets flagged, buy a second and set it as CALLER_ID.

Calling rules to keep in mind

The dialer only calls when you click Call or when its auto-dial countdown runs. Some states, Florida among them, have strict rules for calls placed by automated dialing systems. If you're unsure whether auto-dial is allowed for your leads, turn off Auto-dial in settings so every call needs a click, and check with whoever handles compliance at your company. Call between 8am and 9pm in the contact's time zone, and always honor Do not call requests.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"Could not reach your Twilio Functions"Check the address has no /token on the end, and that you clicked Deploy All after your last change.
"Twilio refused the PIN"The PIN in the dialer must match DIALER_PIN exactly. After changing it in Twilio, click Deploy All again.
"API_KEY and API_SECRET are not set"Add both in Environment Variables (step 6), then Deploy All.
"The browser blocked your microphone"Click the lock icon left of the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, and call again. In Windows, also check Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone.
Calls connect but the other person can't hear youPick your headset as the default microphone in Windows Settings → System → Sound, then reload the dialer.
You hear "This number cannot be dialed"Only US and Canada numbers can be called. Check the number in Edit contact.
