Set up the team CRM and 5pm report

After this, everyone on your team works from one shared list of leads kept in a Google Sheet you own, and the day's call report arrives by email at 5pm. It takes about 15 minutes, done once on a computer.

How it works

The Google Sheet holds your contacts, every call and change, and a log of the daily reports. A short script inside the Sheet, called Apps Script, answers the dialers and sends the email. Each person's dialer keeps a copy of the list, so calling still works with a weak signal. Changes travel to the Sheet within a couple of seconds and show up on your teammates' screens within 30 seconds.

Steps

  1. Make the Google Sheet

    Sign in to the Google account that should own the lead data, such as your work Google account. Open sheets.new to create an empty spreadsheet and name it Call List CRM.

  2. Paste the script

    In the Sheet, choose Extensions → Apps Script. Delete the sample code in Code.gs, paste the code below, and click the Save icon. Name the project Call List CRM when asked.

    Your team key is already filled in.
    Loading…

  3. Check the time zone

    In Apps Script, click Project Settings (the gear on the left) and set Time zone to your own, such as Eastern Time. The 5pm email and the "today" in each report follow this setting.

    If there's no Time zone option, tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor, open appsscript.json from the file list, and change the "timeZone" value, for example to "America/New_York". Then save.

    In the Sheet itself, set the same zone under File → Settings → Time zone so the times in the Activity tab read correctly.

  4. Run setUp once

    Back in the Apps Script editor, pick setUp in the function menu next to Run, then click Run. Google asks for permission:

    • Click Review permissions and choose your account.
    • Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app". This appears for any script you write yourself. Click Advanced, then Go to Call List CRM (unsafe).
    • Click Allow. The script asks to edit this spreadsheet, send email as you (for the report), and run on a schedule.

    When the log says "Execution completed", the Sheet has three new tabs and you'll get a "Your Call List CRM is set up" email.

  5. Deploy it as a web app

    Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to Select type and choose Web app. Set:

    DescriptionDialer
    Execute asMe
    Who has accessAnyone

    Click Deploy, then copy the Web app URL. It starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/ and ends with /exec.

    "Anyone" is needed because the dialer doesn't sign in to Google. Nobody can read or change the list without the team key.

  6. Connect your dialer

    Open the dialer, open Columns & dialing settings, and scroll to Team CRM and 5pm email report. Enter your name, the web app address and the team key, then click Connect.

    The first person to connect chooses Upload my list. This copies the contacts already in your dialer into the Sheet, with every call, note and outcome so far. It takes under a minute for a few hundred leads.

  7. Add your teammates

    Send each teammate the dialer link, the web app address, and the team key, in a private message rather than a group channel. On their phone or computer they open the dialer, enter their own name, the address and the key, click Connect, and choose Use the team list.

    If they already have leads loaded that aren't on the team list, they can choose Add my contacts to it instead. Leads already in the Sheet are skipped: a lead counts as already there when any of its phone numbers, or its name and business together, match one in the Sheet.

  8. Send a test report

    Make a test call, then in Apps Script pick sendReportNow next to Run and click Run. Today's report arrives within a minute. From now on it comes by itself between 5:00 and about 5:15pm, as Google runs scheduled scripts within a 15-minute window.

How the team list works

  • The first call claims a lead. Whoever calls a new lead first becomes its owner, and other people's dialers skip it. Change the owner on the contact's card, or use the Mine and Unassigned filters.
  • Follow-ups come first. When one of your follow-ups is due, the dialer puts that lead at the front of your queue.
  • The top bar shows sync status. "Team synced" means everything is sent. Tap it to sync right away. Offline, it shows how many changes are waiting, and they send by themselves when you're back online.
  • Nothing jumps while you work. Updates never replace notes you're typing or the contact you're on a call with. If a teammate claims the lead on your screen before you call, the dialer moves you to the next one.
  • You can edit the Sheet directly. Change Stage, Owner, follow-up or deal columns in the Contacts tab, and the dialers pick it up on their next sync. Don't rename or move the first 22 columns, and to remove a contact, delete it in the dialer or type TRUE in its Deleted cell instead of deleting the row.
  • Loading another spreadsheet adds to the team list. While connected, Load spreadsheet sends new leads to the Sheet and skips ones already there.
  • The 5pm email covers calls by person, App Outs, connected calls, call backs, deals funded, stage changes, tomorrow's follow-ups and the pipeline, with every call attached as a spreadsheet. A copy of each day's numbers goes in the Daily Reports tab. The dialer's Report tab shows the same report for any day.

Keep the lead data safe

Everything in your lead spreadsheet goes into the Google Sheet, including columns like date of birth and masked SSN or EIN. Share the Sheet itself only with people who need it; teammates using the dialer don't need access to it. If someone leaves the team, make a new key on this page, paste the new code, deploy a new version (below), and give the new key to everyone who stays.

Updating the script later

If the dialer gets a new version of this script, reload this page, use Already set up? Use your existing key so the key stays the same, and copy the code again. In Apps Script, paste it over the old code and save. Then:

  • Click Deploy → Manage deployments, click the pencil icon, set Version to New version, and click Deploy. The web app address stays the same, so nobody has to reconnect.
  • If you changed the time zone or the report time, run setUp again so the 5pm schedule is rebuilt.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"Wrong team key."The key in the dialer must match TEAM_KEY at the top of the script exactly. If you changed the script, deploy a new version.
"Set TEAM_KEY at the top of the Apps Script code"The script was pasted without a key. Copy the code from this page again (the key is filled in), save, and deploy a new version.
"Could not reach your Google Sheet"Check the address ends in /exec, not /dev. In Deploy → Manage deployments, check Who has access is Anyone.
"Google didn't answer like the Call List CRM"Usually Google sent a sign-in page instead. Who has access must be Anyone, not "Anyone with Google account". If your work account doesn't offer "Anyone", your Google admin restricts it; use a personal Google account or ask the admin.
No email at 5pmNo email is sent on days without calls or changes. Check your spam folder. In Apps Script, the Triggers page (clock icon) should list sendDailyReport; if not, run setUp again.
The email comes at the wrong hour, or calls land on the wrong dayFix the time zone (step 3), then run setUp again.
"This app is blocked" when running setUpYour Google Workspace admin blocks scripts that Google hasn't reviewed. Use a personal Google account for the Sheet, or ask the admin to allow it.
A teammate's changes don't showTap the sync status in the top bar on both devices. If one says "Not synced", tap it to see the reason.
